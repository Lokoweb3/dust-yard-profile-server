Checked but not stored

  • Your country. When you use a wallet feature, we look up the country of your IP address with a database on our own server, to follow US sanctions law. The result is used once and not saved.
  • Your connection. To stop one connection from farming matches or drops, we count matches and drops per IP address. The count is kept only in our server's memory, under a scrambled form of the address, and is forgotten within a day.
  • Your age. On your first visit you enter your birth month and year. Only the result (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and older) is kept, in your own browser. Your birthday is never sent to us.

Things that are public by design

Blockchain transactions are public and permanent. Your wallet address, the items and badges it holds, your profile NFT (including your username and stats), and your trades can be seen by anyone on the X1 blockchain. We can't delete them. Your username, scores, chat and market posts are visible to other players.

Who else handles data

  • Hosting: our servers and database run on Railway, which processes traffic, logs and stored data on our behalf.
  • X1 network: to read balances and send transactions, our server and your browser talk to X1 network servers.
  • Other websites you open from the in-game phone have their own privacy policies.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Children

Dust Yard is not for children under 13, and our age check stops them from playing. If we learn that we have information from a child under 13, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at {{CONTACT}}.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without linking a wallet.
  • You can ask us to delete your account information, or ask what we hold about you, at {{CONTACT}}. Information on the blockchain can't be deleted.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional privacy rights; contact us to use them.

Security

We protect our servers and keep the information we hold to a minimum. No system is perfectly secure, so please protect your devices and your wallet recovery phrase.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the game.
