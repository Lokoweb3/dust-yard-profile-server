Dust Yard is a game first. The wallet, swap, market and NFT features are optional and only for players 18 and older. Please read this before you use them.

Transactions can't be undone. Once you approve a swap, purchase, listing or claim in your wallet and it confirms on the X1 blockchain, it is final. We can't reverse it or refund it.
Your recovery phrase is the only way back in. If you lose the 12 words for a wallet, it and everything in it are gone for good. We never see your phrase and we will never ask for it. Anyone who asks for it is a scammer.
Prices move a lot. XNT, USDC.X and NFT prices can rise or fall quickly, and you can lose money. Swaps can fill at a different price than shown (slippage), within the limit shown before you sign.
Items are not investments. Game items are collectibles for playing. They may be worth nothing, there may be no buyer, and their in-game effects may change.
Watch for scams. Check what a transaction does before you sign it. Be careful with links, offers and trades from strangers in chat or the market, and with websites that look like Dust Yard or X1 services.
Technology can fail. Blockchains, wallets, exchanges such as XDEX, and our own servers can have bugs, outages or attacks.
Rules can change. Laws about crypto and NFTs are still changing and may limit or end some features in some places.
Taxes. Trading or swapping may have tax consequences for you. Keep records and ask a tax professional.
